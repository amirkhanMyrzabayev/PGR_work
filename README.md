# Lonely Scientist (OpenGL project)

## Project Description
The scene depicts a remote floating island shrouded in thick fog, inhabited by a lonely scientist. The island features several of his bizarre inventions and interactive elements that the observer can explore. You can encounter a small airplane that starts its flight animation when clicked. The scientist is accompanied by a cat, which visibly shivers due to the cold and inhospitable environment of the island. Another prominent feature is a fully automated, segmented mechanical arm that is constantly at work. There is also a mysterious cube projecting a hologram of the CTU (ČVUT) university logo.

The observer can explore this mysterious island from many different angles. Available camera modes include movement using a free camera, several fixed static views, a smooth fly-through along a predefined spline curve, or a dynamic view from the perspective of the moving airplane. The atmosphere of the scene is enhanced by a day and night cycle with dynamic lighting and fog. For selected objects, you can use the mouse to interactively toggle their animations or attached lights on and off. You can also notice 2D elements, such as animated fire or a time indicator (HUD clock).

## Controls
The application can be controlled using the keyboard and mouse:

* **Mouse:** Look around (while holding the left mouse button). Left-clicking on selected objects (e.g., the airplane) triggers their interaction or animation (picking).
* **W, A, S, D:** Move the free camera (forward, backward, strafe left, and strafe right).
* **Up / Down Arrows:** Move the free camera along the Y-axis (up and down).
* **1:** Switch to the Free Camera.
* **2, 3:** Switch to the Static Camera views.
* **F1:** Start smooth camera animation along a predefined curve (Spline Camera).
* **F2:** Attach the camera to a moving animated object in the scene.
